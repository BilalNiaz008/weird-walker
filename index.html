Make your own walking creature

Weird Walker is a free creature drawing game that brings your doodles to life. Sketch a wobbly monster, a round little blob, or a creature nobody has seen before. Add two legs and watch your drawing shuffle, bounce, or wobble across the stage. You choose the outline, colours, and whether it has a face.

How to make your drawing walk

  1. Draw a body. Use Draw for a freehand outline, drag with Circle to make an oval, or choose Try a blob. To add colour, select Fill and tap inside a closed shape.
  2. Add two legs. Choose Add legs and tap two attachment spots on the body. You can also choose Add face to place eyes and a mouth.
  3. Bring it to life. Name your creature and press Bring it to life. Try a different walk, use Say hello for a reaction, or pause and go Back to drawing to make changes.

Questions about the drawing game

Is Weird Walker free to play?

Yes. You can draw, animate, and save your creature for free. There is no account to create and no app to install.

Can I draw on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Draw and place legs with your finger on a touchscreen, or use a mouse or pen. Weird Walker runs in modern desktop and mobile browsers.

How does my creature choose its walk?

Automatic walking uses your drawing's proportions: tall bodies Wobble, wide bodies Shuffle, and other bodies Bounce. You can choose any of the three walks from the Walk menu.

Can I save my animated drawing?

Use Save image to download a PNG, or Save video for a silent, six-second, 720 × 720 clip. Video downloads as MP4 when supported, otherwise WebM. If your browser cannot record video, you can still save an image.

Where is my creature saved?

Your latest creature is saved in this browser on this device. The app does not upload your drawing. Clearing browser data removes the saved creature, so download an image or video if you want to keep a copy.
