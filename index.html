answers.

A little help for your first creature and its very first steps.

Is Weird Walker free to play?

Yes. You can draw, animate, and save your creature for free. There is no account to create and no app to install.

Can I draw on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Draw and place legs with your finger on a touchscreen, or use a mouse or pen. Weird Walker runs in modern desktop and mobile browsers.

How does my creature choose its walk?

Automatic walking uses your drawing's proportions: tall bodies Wobble, wide bodies Shuffle, and other bodies Bounce. You can choose any of the three walks from the Walk menu.

Can I save my animated drawing?

Use PNG to save a picture, GIF for a looping, six-second animation, or Video for a silent, six-second clip. Videos have a transparent background and download as WebM by default. Turn off Transparent video background to include the stage colour and use MP4 when supported, otherwise WebM. Some video players show transparent areas as black. GIF and image downloads also work when video recording is unavailable.

Where is my creature saved?

Your latest creature is saved in this browser on this device. The app does not upload your drawing. Clearing browser data removes the saved creature, so download an image, GIF, or video if you want to keep a copy.
